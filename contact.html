Common questions

Can’t find your answer? Email us and an engineer will reply.

What does managed IT support include?

A helpdesk for your staff, monitoring of your devices and servers, security updates, backups and a monthly report on what we fixed.

Can you take over our existing Odoo setup?

Yes. We start with a review of your current modules, customisations and data, then take over support or plan an upgrade.

How is pricing structured?

Projects are quoted at a fixed price after an audit. Ongoing support is a monthly plan based on the number of users and devices.

Do we need to replace our current systems?

Usually not. We work with what you have and only recommend replacing things that are unsafe, unsupported or costing you more than they should.
